# EP095 Muse Prompts

## Master character reference
Use the approved explorer character reference exactly as provided by the user.
- olive green hoodie
- beige pants
- gray sneakers
- small brown crossbody bag from right shoulder to left hip
- short black cylindrical flashlight with silver rim
- small black dot eyes, tiny mouth, minimal face
- rounded 3 to 3.5-head-tall proportions
- cute, simple, calm, curious, not baby-like
- keep proportions, clothing, face, bag and flashlight identical in every scene

General style for all scenes:
- vertical 9:16
- approximately 7–8 seconds
- clean cinematic 3D animation blended with realistic urban environments
- no dialogue, no lip sync, no subtitles, no text, no logo, no watermark
- natural movement, not exaggerated
- keep continuity between scenes

## Scene 1 — 퇴근길 군중을 바라보는 탐험가
The approved explorer character stands safely near one side of a busy modern subway platform during evening rush hour. A train has just arrived and many commuters move toward stairs and escalators. The explorer looks surprised at the large crowd, then watches how people keep flowing instead of stopping completely. Camera starts behind the explorer and slowly rises to reveal the movement pattern across the platform. Keep the station realistic and familiar, not futuristic.

## Scene 2 — 여러 갈래로 나뉘는 이동 동선
Continue from the previous scene. The explorer follows the crowd toward a concourse where stairs, escalators, a transfer corridor and several exits branch in different directions. Use a slightly elevated camera angle so the audience can visually understand people splitting into separate flows. Do not use infographic arrows or readable signs. The explorer pauses at the junction and looks from one route to another.

File: scene_002.mp4

## Scene 3 — 좁은 계단에서 생기는 병목
The same explorer observes a station passage where a wide corridor narrows into one staircase and ticket gate area. Show pedestrians moving freely in the wide section, then naturally slowing near the narrower point. Keep the crowd calm and realistic, with no panic. The explorer notices the bottleneck and looks toward the narrow stairway. Camera gently pushes in to emphasize the change in flow speed.

File: scene_003.mp4

## Scene 4 — 환승객과 출구 이용객의 흐름 분리
Show the explorer on an upper concourse overlooking two separate circulation paths: one stream of passengers heads toward a transfer corridor while another stream moves toward exits and ticket gates. Their paths should be physically separated by corridor layout, barriers or different stair locations rather than glowing graphics. The explorer watches from a safe side position as the two groups move without colliding.

File: scene_004.mp4

## Scene 5 — 여러 대피 경로를 가진 역 구조
The environment shifts to a realistic architectural cutaway of the same station while keeping the explorer in the foreground. Show a platform connected to multiple stairs, an escalator bank, a concourse and more than one route toward the surface. Keep the scene focused on spatial layout rather than emergency drama. The explorer traces the possible routes visually, then looks upward toward the surface level.

File: scene_005.mp4

## Scene 6 — 사람의 흐름까지 설계된 공간
Continue naturally into a wide overhead view of the station. Show commuters moving through platform, stairs, concourse and several exits in a steady organized flow. The explorer stands on the concourse and watches the movement from above. Slowly pull the camera back to reveal the station as one connected circulation system. End with a clear visual impression that the architecture is guiding people through the space.

# EP092 Muse Prompts

## Master character reference
Use the approved explorer character reference exactly as provided by the user.
- olive green hoodie
- beige pants
- gray sneakers
- small brown crossbody bag from right shoulder to left hip
- short black cylindrical flashlight with silver rim
- small black dot eyes, tiny mouth, minimal face
- rounded 3 to 3.5-head-tall proportions
- cute, simple, calm, curious, not baby-like
- keep proportions, clothing, face, bag and flashlight identical in every scene

General style for all scenes:
- vertical 9:16
- approximately 7–8 seconds
- clean cinematic 3D animation blended with realistic urban environments
- no dialogue, no lip sync, no subtitles, no text, no logo, no watermark
- natural movement, not exaggerated
- keep continuity between scenes

## Scene 1 — 끝없이 내려가는 에스컬레이터
The approved explorer character enters a modern underground subway station and steps onto a very long descending escalator. The camera begins behind the character, slightly above shoulder height. The explorer looks down as the escalator continues much farther than expected, then tilts their head in curiosity. Other commuters are sparse and secondary. The station should feel familiar and realistic, not futuristic. End with the explorer still descending and looking toward another lower level ahead.

## Scene 2 — 도시 지하 단면 발견 (revised)
Continue naturally from the long escalator descent. The explorer reaches a lower landing and pauses beside a large glass-covered engineering display built into the station wall. Inside the display is a realistic physical cutaway model of the city underground, shown as stacked layers rather than a magical transformation. At the top are a street and building foundations. Below them are utility pipes and sewer lines. Deeper down is an older subway tunnel, and beneath that a newer tunnel running at a lower level. The explorer leans closer, studies the model, then points toward the deepest tunnel with a curious expression. Start with a medium shot of the explorer at the display, then slowly push the camera closer to the underground layers. Keep the model visually clear, physically believable, and simple enough to understand at a glance. Avoid floating infographic lines, glowing diagrams, labels, or text. End with the camera focused on the deepest tunnel layer so Scene 3 can continue from that idea.

## Scene 3 — 기존 시설을 피해 더 깊게
The same explorer now observes a simplified underground construction visualization. A new subway tunnel route approaches existing buried utilities and an older rail tunnel, then curves gently deeper beneath them instead of intersecting them. Show the tunnel path as physical excavation space rather than bright infographic lines. Camera slowly tracks downward with the route. The explorer watches from a safe observation platform or cutaway foreground.

File: scene_003.mp4

## Scene 4 — 깊이는 여러 조건으로 정해진다
Show the explorer studying a physical subway planning model on a table in a small station engineering room. The model contains terrain elevation, building foundations, underground pipes and two possible tunnel depths. The explorer compares the two routes with a thoughtful expression. Then briefly transition to a cross-section showing the chosen route passing safely below existing structures. No readable labels or text.

File: scene_004.mp4

## Scene 5 — 깊은 터널의 장점과 새로운 문제
The same explorer walks beside a deep underground metro tunnel service corridor. Show thick tunnel walls, a ventilation shaft rising upward, emergency stairs and maintenance access in the background. Briefly visualize a train passing in the deep tunnel while the surface above remains calm, with only subtle vibration. The explorer looks upward at the long ventilation shaft, emphasizing how deep infrastructure also requires more complex ventilation and evacuation systems.

File: scene_005.mp4

## Scene 6 — 플랫폼 도착, 위에 겹쳐진 도시
Continue naturally. The explorer finally arrives at a deep subway platform. The camera starts beside the explorer, then slowly pulls upward into a stylized cutaway showing the platform far below the street, with several layers above: another tunnel, utilities, building foundations, roads and the city surface. The explorer looks upward while holding the flashlight at their side. End on the layered city cross-section, creating the feeling that the subway is one layer inside a much older and more complex city.

# EP094 Muse Prompts

## Master character reference
Use the approved explorer character reference exactly as provided by the user.
- olive green hoodie
- beige pants
- gray sneakers
- small brown crossbody bag from right shoulder to left hip
- short black cylindrical flashlight with silver rim
- small black dot eyes, tiny mouth, minimal face
- rounded 3 to 3.5-head-tall proportions
- cute, simple, calm, curious, not baby-like
- keep proportions, clothing, face, bag and flashlight identical in every scene

General style for all scenes:
- vertical 9:16
- approximately 7–8 seconds
- clean cinematic 3D animation blended with realistic urban environments
- no dialogue, no lip sync, no subtitles, no text, no logo, no watermark
- natural movement, not exaggerated
- keep continuity between scenes
- use believable subway infrastructure, not sci-fi corridors

## Scene 1 — 승강장 끝의 닫힌 문
Continue the subway mini-series with the same approved explorer character. The explorer stands near the far end of a modern subway platform, at a safe distance from the track. They notice a plain restricted staff door and a dim service passage beyond the public area. The explorer leans slightly forward with curiosity and raises the flashlight, but does not enter a dangerous area. Camera slowly pushes toward the closed door. End on the explorer looking at the door.

## Scene 2 — 문 뒤의 작업 공간
Continue from the same door. Transition into a safe cutaway view that reveals what can exist behind public station walls: a narrow staff corridor, electrical cabinets, communication equipment, maintenance storage and a passage leading toward rail infrastructure. The explorer remains in a designated observation area and points toward the hidden service spaces. Everything should look functional, industrial and realistic, not secret-laboratory-like.

File: scene_002.mp4

## Scene 3 — 터널 옆의 좁은 통로
The explorer now observes a realistic subway tunnel from a protected cutaway viewpoint. Show rails, tunnel wall, cables, emergency lighting and a narrow maintenance or evacuation walkway beside the track. A train passes through briefly while the explorer watches from behind a safety barrier. Emphasize that the tunnel contains more than just rails.

File: scene_003.mp4

## Scene 4 — 역마다 다른 뒷공간
Show the explorer looking at a physical 3D station model in a small engineering room. The model reveals different possible spaces behind a platform: staff rooms, equipment rooms, connecting passages and tunnel access. Then transition briefly between two different station layouts to show that no single hidden-space layout applies to every station. No readable labels.

File: scene_004.mp4

## Scene 5 — 역을 움직이는 설비
The explorer walks through an authorized service corridor with a staff guide implied but not prominently shown. Reveal ventilation ducts, drainage pipes, pumps, electrical equipment and signal cabinets behind the public-facing station walls. Fans rotate slowly and water moves through a drain channel. Keep the scene visually understandable and grounded in real infrastructure.

File: scene_005.mp4

## Scene 6 — 벽 하나 뒤의 또 다른 지하철
Return to the public platform. The explorer stands beside the same restricted door from Scene 1. The camera pulls backward, then transitions into a wide cutaway view of the station showing the public platform in front and the network of service corridors, equipment rooms and tunnel infrastructure behind it. The explorer looks around with a small curious expression. End with the sense that the visible platform is only one part of a much larger underground system.

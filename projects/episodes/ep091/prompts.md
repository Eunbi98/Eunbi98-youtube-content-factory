# EP091 Muse Prompts

## Topic
일부 주민만 들었다는 정체불명의 저주파음, 타오스 험

## Common rules
Photorealistic. Documentary style. Natural motion. No dialogue. No narration. No text. No subtitles. No logo. No watermark. Vertical 9:16. Approximately 8 seconds.

## Scene 1 — Hook
A quiet small town in Taos, New Mexico at night in the early 1990s, adobe houses, desert mountain landscape, a few dim streetlights, one resident standing still outside as if noticing a distant low mechanical hum. The camera slowly pushes toward the person while the town remains visually calm. Mysterious but realistic atmosphere, not horror, no visible source of the sound, natural history and investigative documentary style, vertical 9:16, about 8 seconds, no text, no logo.

## Scene 2 — Only some people hear it
Inside a quiet bedroom at night, one adult resident suddenly looks toward the wall as if hearing a faint low-frequency vibration, while another person nearby remains completely unaffected and continues reading. Subtle realistic acting, no supernatural effects, no visible sound waves, slow lateral camera movement, muted nighttime lighting, investigative documentary style, vertical 9:16, about 8 seconds, no text, no logo.

A small interdisciplinary research team sets up bulky acoustic measuring equipment inside and outside a quiet house, including large microphones, spectrum analyzers and recording devices. Researchers carefully check instruments and interview a resident. Period-appropriate equipment, realistic scientific procedure, slow handheld documentary camera, vertical 9:16, about 8 seconds, no text, no logo.

The instruments show activity but no dramatic single source is revealed. Avoid fake labels or readable numbers. Present these only as possible explanations, not confirmed causes. No conspiracy imagery, no secret-agent aesthetic, no supernatural effects.

# EP093 Muse Prompts

## Master character reference
Use the approved explorer character reference exactly as provided by the user.
- olive green hoodie
- beige pants
- gray sneakers
- small brown crossbody bag from right shoulder to left hip
- short black cylindrical flashlight with silver rim
- small black dot eyes, tiny mouth, minimal face
- rounded 3 to 3.5-head-tall proportions
- cute, simple, calm, curious, not baby-like
- keep proportions, clothing, face, bag and flashlight identical in every scene

General style for all scenes:
- vertical 9:16
- approximately 7–8 seconds
- clean cinematic 3D animation blended with realistic subway environments
- no dialogue, no lip sync, no subtitles, no text, no logo, no watermark
- natural movement, not exaggerated
- keep continuity between scenes
- no graphic injury, no panic crowd, no disaster-movie exaggeration

## Scene 1 — 연기는 어디로 갈까?
The approved explorer character stands on a modern underground subway platform. A small controlled amount of gray smoke begins appearing far down the tunnel, with no visible flames and no panic. The explorer notices it, pauses, and looks upward toward the ceiling vents with a curious but serious expression. Camera starts behind the character and slowly moves closer. End with the explorer looking toward a ventilation grille above.

## Scene 2 — 숨은 환기 시스템
Continue from the previous scene. The ceiling and wall smoothly transition into a clean cutaway visualization showing hidden ventilation ducts behind the station surfaces. Show exhaust fans, supply-air passages and vertical shafts leading toward the surface. The explorer remains in the foreground and points toward the hidden air route. Keep the system plausible and mechanical, not futuristic, with no readable labels.

File: scene_002.mp4

## Scene 3 — 팬의 방향이 달라지는 이유
The same explorer watches a simplified tunnel control demonstration. Show a subway tunnel with a train stopped at a safe distance from a small smoke source. Large ventilation fans at different points activate in sequence, causing the smoke to move away from a designated evacuation direction. Use visible but realistic airflow through subtle moving haze, not glowing arrows. The explorer studies a control panel with no readable text.

File: scene_003.mp4

## Scene 4 — 대피로를 지키는 공기
Continue into a station stairwell and emergency passage. The explorer walks toward the stairs while light smoke remains behind a closed boundary and cleaner air is maintained in the escape route. Show doors, smoke barriers and pressurized airflow subtly preventing smoke from entering the stair zone. Keep the scene calm, instructional and realistic, with no frightened crowd.

File: scene_004.mp4

## Scene 5 — 승강장 뒤에 숨어 있는 것들
The explorer walks through a maintenance-side cutaway beside the platform. Reveal hidden exhaust ducts, under-platform air passages, a vertical ventilation shaft and an emergency staircase behind the public-facing station walls. The camera tracks beside the explorer as each hidden element appears naturally in the environment. Emphasize that much of the safety infrastructure is normally invisible to passengers.

File: scene_005.mp4

## Scene 6 — 지하철역은 공기의 길이기도 하다
The explorer returns to the normal platform after the smoke has cleared. Camera slowly pulls backward and upward into a stylized station cutaway, showing the platform, tunnel, ventilation ducts and shafts connecting toward the surface. The explorer looks up at the layered airflow system while holding the flashlight at their side. End with a calm wide view of the station as a complex network for both people and air.

# EP096 Muse Prompts

## Master character reference
Use the approved explorer character reference exactly as provided by the user.
- olive green hoodie
- beige pants
- gray sneakers
- small brown crossbody bag from right shoulder to left hip
- short black cylindrical flashlight with silver rim
- small black dot eyes, tiny mouth, minimal face
- rounded 3 to 3.5-head-tall proportions
- cute, simple, calm, curious, not baby-like
- keep proportions, clothing, face, bag and flashlight identical in every scene

General style for all scenes:
- vertical 9:16
- approximately 7–8 seconds
- clean cinematic 3D animation blended with realistic urban environments
- no dialogue, no lip sync, no subtitles, no text, no logo, no watermark
- natural movement, not exaggerated
- keep continuity between scenes
- avoid futuristic sci-fi architecture unless specifically needed

## Scene 1 — 빌딩 꼭대기 안쪽의 거대한 추
The approved explorer character rides an elevator to the upper mechanical levels of a modern supertall skyscraper and enters a safe observation area behind glass. The explorer notices a huge suspended metal mass inside the building, hanging from thick cables or supports. The scale should feel surprising: the explorer is tiny compared with the enormous damper. Camera slowly reveals the full size of the mass. End with the explorer looking upward at it in curiosity.

## Scene 2 — 바람에 아주 조금 움직이는 건물
Continue with the same skyscraper. Show an exterior cinematic view of the tall building in strong wind, with clouds moving quickly. The motion of the building should be subtle and realistic, not visibly bending like rubber. Transition to the explorer standing safely near an upper-floor window, noticing a hanging object or cup of water move slightly. Emphasize that tall buildings can sway gently in wind.

File: scene_002.mp4

## Scene 3 — 반대 방향으로 움직이는 추
Show a simplified cutaway of the skyscraper. The same explorer watches from a safe interior platform while the building moves slightly to one side and the large suspended mass shifts in the opposite direction. Keep the movement slow, physically plausible, and easy to understand without arrows or text. The camera should frame both the building motion and the damper response in one continuous shot.

File: scene_003.mp4

## Scene 4 — 건물의 흔들림에 맞춰 조정되는 장치
The explorer examines a small physical engineering model of the skyscraper and its suspended mass damper in a maintenance room. A technician-free demonstration model gently oscillates. Show two short visual moments: first the mass moving poorly out of sync, then a properly tuned version reducing the model's motion. No readable screens or labels. Keep the explanation visual and intuitive.

File: scene_004.mp4

## Scene 5 — 건물마다 다른 감쇠 방식
Show the explorer walking through a stylized but plausible structural engineering gallery inside the skyscraper. Present several non-text visual models: a suspended mass damper, a compact mechanical damper between structural members, and another damping system integrated into the frame. The explorer compares them while the camera moves laterally. Make clear that different tall buildings can use different systems.

File: scene_005.mp4

## Scene 6 — 도시 위에서 바라보는 결론
The explorer stands safely at a high observation window at dusk, looking across the city skyline. The camera slowly pulls back to reveal the upper floors and the hidden damper inside the building behind the walls in a subtle cutaway. The building moves almost imperceptibly in the wind while the damper responds inside. End with a calm wide shot of the skyscraper above the city.
